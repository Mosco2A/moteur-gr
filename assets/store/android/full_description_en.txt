StepWays guides you along your long-distance trail, from preparation to the final stage. No account to create: the app opens and can be explored straight away, with a free demo.

The trail's stages, track, points of interest and emergency numbers are stored on your phone. Without a signal, your position, the track and the off-trail alerts keep working.

PREPARE YOUR TREK
• The trail split into days, with a suggested programme based on your feasibility test
• Detailed stages: distance, elevation gain and loss, estimated walking time
• Accommodation listed along the route
• Gear checklist to tick off, adapted to the season
• Tip sheets and a training plan

GPS GUIDANCE
• Your position and progress in real time on the map
• An alert when you stray from the trail, cleared as soon as you are back on it
• Distance walked, cumulative elevation gain, walking time and speed, live

MAP AND POINTS OF INTEREST
• Trail map with the track and the stages
• Points of interest: water sources, shelters, viewpoints, shops
• The map background loads over the network

WEATHER AND SAFETY
• Weather for each stage: today and the next two days
• Wildfire risk level for each stage, calculated from the weather
• SOS button that calls 112
• Regional emergency numbers, specific to each trail
• Emergency sheet (health, people to contact) kept on your phone

TRAIL JOURNAL AND MEMORIES
• Trail journal with your notes and photos, sorted by stage
• Your completed treks stay in "My treks"
• Personalised finisher diploma, ready to share
• Export your track as a GPX file
• Share your plan, journal or diploma in one tap

PRIVACY
• No account to create, no name or email address requested
• Your photos and journal stay on your phone
• No location data used for advertising

Each trail comes with its own stages, points of interest and regional emergency numbers. The app is available in French, English, German, Italian and Spanish, in dark or light theme.

Try the free demo, then choose your trail.
