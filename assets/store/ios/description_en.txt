The Ways turns your hike into a guided experience, from the first scouting to the final step — fully offline.

Download a trail and set off without a signal: the map, GPS track, planner and journal stay available wherever you are.

PLAN YOUR TREK
• Planning tailored to your level, your dates and your pace
• Detailed stages: distances, elevation gain and estimated walking time
• Interactive elevation profiles for every stage
• Accommodation options listed along the route

ACCURATE GPS GUIDANCE
• High-definition GPX track to follow the right path
• Real-time tracking of your progress on the map
• Off-route alerts to stay on the trail
• Works 100% offline, no network required

OFFLINE MAPS
• Maps downloadable by sector, usable without a connection
• Points of interest: water sources, shelters, viewpoints
• Compact data to spare your device's storage

PROGRESS TRACKING
• Statistics for your ongoing trek
• Distance covered, cumulative elevation, walking time
• History of your past hikes
• Personalised completion certificate

SHARING AND TRAIL JOURNAL
• Share your progress with your loved ones in real time
• Trail journal illustrated with geolocated photos
• Export your tracks in GPX format

BUILT FOR THE FIELD
• Battery-saving mode
• Display readable in bright sunlight
• Designed for areas with no network coverage

PRIVACY
• Your photos and journal stay on your device
• Your location is shared only when you enable it, via a private link
• No location data used for advertising

The Ways adapts to every trail: colours, stages, points of interest and regional emergency numbers are supplied by the route's configuration. Whether you are preparing your first long hike or you are a seasoned walker, the app supports you before, during and after the adventure.
